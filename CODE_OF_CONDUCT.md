# Code of conduct

Whetstone is a practice tool for people preparing for job applications, often under pressure. Be kind
to them and to each other.

- Be respectful, patient and constructive, in issues, pull requests and discussions alike.
- Assume good faith. A report of a wrong question is a gift, even when the question turns out to be right.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, and no discrimination of any kind.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block participants, that break these rules. To raise a
concern privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
